# Welcome to Nexova Solutions

## AI Engineering · 4Geeks Academy — Company Briefing

---

Nexova Solutions is an HR consulting and talent acquisition company founded in 2011, headquartered in Valencia, Spain, with an expansion office in Miami, Florida. The company has 120 employees and generates around 8 million dollars in annual revenue across three business lines: headhunting for mid-level and executive profiles, outsourced customer support teams for tech companies, and corporate training in soft skills and leadership.

Nexova's clients are mid-sized companies, mainly in tech, retail, and financial services, that have chosen to outsource part or all of their talent management operations. They rely on Nexova because finding the right people, training them, and keeping them engaged is difficult, and Nexova has spent twelve years building the expertise to do it well.

## How the company is organized

Nexova is led by Laura Mendoza, CEO since the company's foundation. She built the business from a two-person consultancy into a 120-employee firm operating in two countries. She is direct, results-driven, and has a clear vision of what Nexova needs to become. What she lacks are the right tools to get there.

The company is structured into the following areas:

Recruitment Operations is the core of Nexova's business and its main revenue source. Javier Almeida manages a team of 40 recruitment consultants who run hiring processes for clients, from the initial brief to CV screening, interviews, and final hiring. Every step in this process is currently manual.

Corporate Training designs and delivers training programs for client companies. Elena Vargas leads a team of 12 people who manage courses, workshops, and webinars on leadership, communication, and team management. The catalog exists and enrollments happen, but there is barely any infrastructure behind it: a PDF, a Google Form, and a spreadsheet.

Outsourced Customer Support is where Nexova provides dedicated service teams to clients. Roberto Diaz manages 30 agents who handle incidents for technology, retail, and finance companies through phone, email, and chat. The team is capable, but it operates without a knowledge base, without real-time visibility, and with resolution times that regularly miss committed service levels.

Sales and Business Development drives company growth. Megan Clarke leads a team of 18 people: account managers who care for existing clients and SDRs pursuing new ones. The team has a CRM but uses it inconsistently, most outreach is manual, and they lose deals they should not lose simply because follow-up is forgotten.

Marketing and Communications manages Nexova's external presence: the corporate website, LinkedIn, the monthly newsletter, and the blog. Carmen Ruiz leads a small team that produces everything manually and has limited visibility into what actually works.

Human Resources manages everything related to Nexova's 120 internal employees: contracts, onboarding, vacations, performance reviews, and day-to-day requests. Patricia Solis runs this with a four-person team, mostly via email and spreadsheets.

Technology and Infrastructure is led by CTO Sergio Molina, who has a six-person team. The current tech landscape is a collection of tools introduced one by one to solve isolated problems and never properly integrated: a legacy helpdesk, a CRM, an in-house applicant tracking system built in the early 2010s, and Google Workspace as connective tissue for everything else. There is no telemetry, no centralized logging, and no clear real-time view of business operations.

Executive Management centers on Laura, who receives a weekly PDF report manually prepared by each department leader, a process that consumes hours every week and still delivers information that is already several days old when it arrives.

## Where the company is today

Nexova has a strong market reputation, a deep candidate and client network built over twelve years, and a team that truly knows how to find, develop, and support talent. What it does not have is the operational infrastructure to scale.

The consequences are tangible: consultants spend most of their time on manual tasks that could be automated, clients have no visibility into hiring process status, the training catalog is invisible to most potential buyers, support teams miss SLAs, and executives make decisions with data that is always one week behind.

New platforms, automated, data-driven, and built to scale, are starting to compete directly with what Nexova offers. Laura knows the response window is limited.

She has assembled an AI Engineering team to build the systems, automations, and intelligent tools that allow Nexova to keep doing what it already does well, but faster, at greater scale, and with far less manual effort.

You are part of that team.

## Departments and their problems

### Marketing and Communications

Owner: Carmen Ruiz

The corporate website was built in 2019 and has not been significantly updated. It is slow, not accessible, and does not reflect the company's current positioning. Content production is fully manual, and the team does not measure the real impact of its work: they know visits, but not what converts.

What they need: A full website redesign with SEO/GEO optimization and schema.org markup, an AI-assisted content pipeline, and a marketing metrics dashboard with visits, traffic sources, and conversion rates.

---

### Sales and Business Development

Owner: Marcos Ibanez (18-person team: 6 account managers, 12 SDRs)

SDRs prospect manually on LinkedIn, export lists to Excel, and follow up through email without automation. They use HubSpot, but only about 40% of the team updates it consistently. Many deals are lost due to missing follow-up, and there is no visibility into which prospects are most likely to close.

What they need: A sales pipeline dashboard, prospecting sequence automation (first contact to follow-up to close/discard), inactivity alerts for stalled conversations, and an AI agent that suggests the best proposal angle for each prospect.

---

### Internal Human Resources

Owner: Patricia Solis

Vacation requests, absences, and HR questions are managed by email and spreadsheets. New employee onboarding is manual. Performance reviews happen twice a year through Google Forms that are not systematically reviewed. There are no tracking metrics: no turnover rate, no absenteeism data, no average time to fill internal roles.

What they need: An internal HR portal, an automated onboarding flow with progressive checklists, an HR KPI dashboard, and an internal agent that answers policy and benefits questions.

---

### Recruitment Operations (core business)

Owner: Javier Almeida (40 recruitment consultants)

CV screening is manual: each consultant reads 30 to 80 CVs per process. Candidate communication happens through one-to-one emails without templates. There is no system to track each candidate in real time, so clients call to ask for updates. Candidate-to-role matching depends entirely on consultant intuition.

What they need: An AI-assisted recruitment pipeline with automatic CV scoring and ranking, a RAG system over the candidate database (for example, find candidates with B2B sales experience and C1 English), a candidate portal with real-time status, and a communication agent that sends automated follow-up emails.

---

### Corporate Training

Owner: Elena Vargas (12-person team)

The training catalog lives in a PDF updated quarterly. Enrollments are managed through a Google Form and then manually administered in a spreadsheet. There is no reliable record of who completed what beyond Excel attendance sheets. Personalization is zero: every client gets the same catalog proposal.

What they need: A searchable training catalog platform with online enrollment, a recommendation system that suggests programs based on client profile, a learner portal with progress tracking, and a training advisory chatbot that builds personalized plans.

---

### Customer Support (outsourced service)

Owner: Roberto Diaz (30 agents)

The 30 agents handle incidents for Nexova's outsourcing clients (tech, retail, finance) by phone, email, and web chat. There is no centralized knowledge base. Agents solve issues based on personal experience plus a shared Word document in Drive. Average resolution time is 48 hours while the committed SLA is 24 hours. Supervisors have no real-time visibility into workload or backlog status.

What they need: A first-line support chatbot that resolves 40% of inquiries without human intervention using RAG, a centralized semantic-search knowledge base, a real-time support dashboard, and ticket sentiment analysis to identify dissatisfied clients before escalation.

---

### Technology and Infrastructure

CTO: Sergio Molina (6-person team)

The tech stack is a mosaic of disconnected tools: HubSpot, legacy Zendesk, Google Workspace, a custom ATS from the 2010s, and multiple spreadsheets acting as management systems. There is no telemetry or centralized logging. When something fails, the team learns through users. Deployments are manual.

What they need: Centralized telemetry and logging, a data pipeline feeding every company dashboard, real-time monitoring with automatic alerts, and an internal engineering agent that answers architecture questions by consulting technical docs.

---

### Executive Management

CEO: Laura Mendoza

Laura receives a weekly PDF report manually prepared by each area manager. Preparation takes between 4 and 8 hours per manager. There is no unified business view. Strategic decisions are made using week-old data.

What she needs: A unified executive dashboard with real-time KPIs from all areas, an automatically generated weekly report, strategic threshold alerts, and an AI assistant she can query in natural language.

## Why choose Nexova?

Choose Nexova if you are interested in:

- People and data: building systems that rank candidates, track learners, and measure sales performance.
- B2B services: your users are not anonymous consumers but professional teams with concrete workflows and expectations.
- AI at the business core: in Nexova, AI is not a side feature, it is the competitive advantage. CV scoring, candidate matching, RAG over knowledge bases, and first-line support agents are core deliverables.
- Cross-functional complexity: you will build for five very different teams (operations, sales, HR, training, support), each with different data, users, and success metrics.

AI challenges at Nexova include semantic search over candidate databases, first-line support automation that must respect SLAs, explainable CV scoring and ranking pipelines, and recommendation systems for training programs. If you want your portfolio to demonstrate AI Engineering applied directly to a company's core operations, not only internal tooling, Nexova is your company.
